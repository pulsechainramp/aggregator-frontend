# FAQ

## When will my bridge finish?

Most transfers take 3-10 minutes depending on network congestion. The status panel shows each milestone so you know when to expect the claim step.

## Can I bridge directly from an exchange?

No. Always withdraw to a self-custodial wallet first, then use that wallet to interact with the bridge.

## Which wallets are supported?

Any EVM-compatible wallet that works with your browser or device (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Frame, etc.).

## How do referrals work?

Enter a referral code on the landing page or follow a referral link. Eligible fees are displayed before you confirm a swap or bridge.

## Who do I contact for help?

Use the support links in the footer, join the PulseChainRamp community channels, or open the Troubleshooting doc for self-serve fixes.
