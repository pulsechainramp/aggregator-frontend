# Fees & Limits

## What You Pay

- **Onramp providers**: display card/bank processing fees before checkout.
- **Bridge gas**: paid on the origin chain (typically Ethereum) using ETH.
- **PulseChain gas**: a small amount of PLS is needed to claim and swap.
- **Service fees**: shown clearly in the UI when referrals or promotions apply.

## Limits

- Minimum onramp purchase often starts around $20 USD (varies by provider).
- Bridge minimums depend on the token; expect ~10 USD equivalent.
- Maximums follow compliance requirements per provider and per region.

## Tips

- Send slightly more than your intended swap amount to cover gas on both chains.
- Use lower-cost payment rails (ACH, SEPA) when timing is flexible.
