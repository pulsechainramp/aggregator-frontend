# Safety & Trust

> ### Key Points
> - PulseChainRamp is non-custodial; you keep control of your assets and keys.
> - We never ask for your recovery phrase or remote access.
> - Status banners and support links notify you about incidents in real time.

**Steps**
1. Bookmark the official site and only connect your wallet there.
2. Double-check the network indicator before approving any transaction.
3. Read slippage, fee, and referral notices before clicking confirm.
4. Use the Docs sidebar for deeper help without leaving the app.
5. Report anything suspicious through Contact Us or the community channels.

**Common mistakes**
- Trusting messages claiming to be support or asking for seed phrases.
- Bridging directly from an exchange wallet.
- Rushing through approvals without confirming the token and chain.

**Troubleshooting**
- Unsure about a prompt? Cancel it; real opportunities do not expire.
- Think you clicked a phishing link? Disconnect, revoke approvals, and rotate wallets if needed.
- Transaction feels wrong? Compare with historical gas prices or pause until you are comfortable.
