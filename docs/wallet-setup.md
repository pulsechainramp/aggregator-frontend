# Wallet Setup

- MetaMask (desktop/mobile)
- Coinbase Wallet (mobile)
- Rabby (desktop)
- Write down your recovery phrase. Never share it.
- Add the PulseChain network plus common tokens (PLS, WPLS, USDC, DAI).

## Tips

- Use a fresh browser profile or app to avoid old approvals.
- Double-check that your wallet is pointed at Ethereum before you initiate a bridge deposit.
- Keep a small ETH balance for gas on the origin chain and a bit of PLS for gas once you land.
