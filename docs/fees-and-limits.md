# Fees & Limits

> ### Key Points
> - You pay Ethereum gas, PulseChain gas, and any bridge/swap fees shown in the UI.
> - Referral fees cap at 3% and are always disclosed before you confirm.

**What you'll need**
- Wallet on Ethereum  /  ETH or stablecoins  /  Awareness of local onramp fees

**Steps**
1. Check the Onramp widget for processing fees (card, ACH, SEPA).
2. Leave extra ETH for gas before sending everything.
3. Review the bridge estimate, which includes protocol fees plus gas.
4. On PulseChain, keep a small PLS stash to cover swaps and approvals.

**Common mistakes**
- Moving the exact dollar amount needed and forgetting gas overhead.
- Assuming fees are flat; they scale based on token and congestion.

**Troubleshooting**
- Fee looks too high? Refresh quotes or wait for gas to settle.
- Unsure about the final amount? Test with a small transfer first.
